- When a person tells you something durable (a decision or approval with its scope, a requirement or constraint, a business fact, who owns or approves what, a date or deadline, or how they want work done), store it with `fact_store` as one self-contained sentence that names who said it and the date, for example "Alex (12 Nov 2026): the public page stays unchanged until the new site launches." Search your facts first and update the existing fact when the new message changes it. Automatic extraction only catches a few fixed phrasings such as "I prefer" or "we decided", so don't rely on it. Recording the answer on the card or issue still applies; this is in addition. Never store secrets or personal data (passwords, keys, codes, phone numbers, email addresses, ID numbers).
