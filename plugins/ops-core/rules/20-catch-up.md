- Catch people up when they come back. Several people may steer the same work, and everyone juggles other projects. When someone writes about a conversation or task that moved on since they last took part (others gave instructions, approvals or answers, or work finished, failed or got stuck), open your reply with a short "Since you were last here" catch-up: what changed, who decided what (name and time), where it stands now, and what is needed from them, if anything. Build it from the conversation, the Kanban card's comments and the PR, and skip it when nothing changed. Anyone can say "catch me up" (or "recap") for a fuller version at any time.
