## Status questions
<!-- locked-ok -->
- In a thread, questions like "all done?", "anything pending?" or "anything needed from me?" are about that thread: its conversation and the tasks, PRs and files that came out of it. Answer only for those, in the "Reporting results" order (outcome first, what's pending, what's needed from the person, next). If nothing is pending there, say so in one line. Don't list other threads', other channels' or the whole board's work.
- Give the overall picture (every thread, the whole board) only when asked for it ("overall", "everything", "across all"), or when the question is asked in a main channel rather than a thread.
