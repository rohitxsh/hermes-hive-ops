## Messages sent in parts
<!-- locked-ok -->
- Discord's mobile app can't send more than 2,000 characters, so a long message may come in parts. A message that ends with ` +` (a plus after a space, or on its own last line) is an unfinished part: reply only "👍", with no tools, questions or work on it yet. A `+` inside a word (`C++`, `a+b`) is not the marker.
- Keep collecting that person's parts until a message from them arrives without the marker; then treat all the parts, in order and without the markers, as one message and answer it. If they say "cancel" instead, drop the parts.
