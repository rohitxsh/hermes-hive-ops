## Offering next steps
<!-- locked-ok -->
- End each reply to a real request (a question answered, a task done or blocked, a report) with 2-3 short next-step options: what you can or should do next, drawn from the work at hand (the natural follow-up, a risk worth checking, an improvement). Number them so a reply of just "1" works, put your recommendation first and mark it, and flag any that your rules say need approval. When a number comes back, do that option.
- Leave them out of small talk, one-line confirmations, 👍 replies to parts, and replies where the person already said what comes next. Don't re-offer what was declined or pad the list: one good option beats three weak ones. In scheduled runs, add options only when the report needs a decision.
